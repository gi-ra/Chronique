Client Services

Client Services

Have a question about an order, a return, or anything else? Here's what we can answer straight away — and if you don't see it below, email us at maison@chronique.au and we'll get back to you.

Order status

How do I check the status of my order?
We don't use customer accounts, so there's no order history to log into. You'll get an order confirmation email right after checkout, and a second email with tracking details as soon as your order ships. If you can't find either, check your spam folder first, then email us with your order details and we'll look it up for you.
Can I cancel or change my order?
Email us as soon as possible after ordering. If it hasn't been packed yet, we can usually cancel it or update the shipping address — once it's shipped, our standard returns policy applies instead.
I haven't received an order confirmation email.
Check your spam or junk folder first. Still nothing? Email us at maison@chronique.au and we'll resend it.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay at checkout.
My payment won't go through. What should I do?
This is usually something your bank or card issuer can resolve quickly — they can confirm whether the payment was declined or just delayed. If it keeps happening, email us and we'll help however we can.
Can a discount code be applied after I've already ordered?
No — discount codes and promotions can only be applied at checkout and can't be added retroactively to a completed order.

Shipping & delivery

How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll get an email with your tracking number. Processing normally takes a few business days before dispatch — this can run a little longer during busy periods or restocks.
What are my shipping options?
Shipping is calculated automatically from your address at checkout:

Free local delivery — if you're within 100km of Logan Central QLD 4114, we hand-deliver your order ourselves within 3 business days, at no cost. (PO Box and Parcel Locker addresses can't be hand-delivered, so these get the Rest of Australia rate below instead.)

Rest of Australia — a flat $10 AUD, free on orders of $150 AUD or more.

New Zealand — a flat $20 AUD.

Rest of the world — a flat $30 AUD. International orders may be charged import duties or taxes by your country, payable by you on delivery.
What if my order hasn't arrived?
If tracking shows your order as delivered but it hasn't turned up, please wait 48 hours in case it's still on its way, then email us and we'll help sort it out.

Returns & exchanges

What is your return policy?
We want you to be happy with what you've ordered. Unworn items in original condition, with tags attached, can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.
How do I start a return?
Email us at maison@chronique.au with your order number and what you'd like to return. We'll confirm the details and talk you through sending it back.
How long does a refund take?
Once we've received and checked your return, please allow 5–7 business days for your refund to be processed back to your original payment method.
What if my item arrives damaged or faulty?
Email us photos of the issue within 60 days of purchase and we'll sort out a replacement or refund.

Sale policy

Can I return an item bought on sale?
Items marked as final sale can't be returned or exchanged. Anything not marked final sale follows our standard returns policy above.
Can I get a price adjustment if an item I bought goes on sale later?
We're not able to offer retroactive price adjustments — discounts only apply to orders placed while a promotion is active.

Products & care

How do I care for my Chronique pieces?
Care instructions are listed on each product page. As a general rule: machine wash cold, inside out, and lay flat to dry to keep the shape and colour.
Will a sold-out item be restocked?
Sometimes — restocks aren't guaranteed and sell on a first-come, first-served basis. Use the "Notify me" option on a sold-out size or colour and we'll email you the moment it's back.
What if I receive a damaged or faulty product?
Email us photos of the issue within 60 days of purchase and we'll take care of it.

Drops & restocks

How do I find out about new drops?
Subscribe to our mailing list and follow us on Instagram — that's where new arrivals and restocks are announced first.
Do you hold items or run raffles for limited releases?
No — limited pieces are released directly to the shop and sell on a first-come, first-served basis. We aren't able to hold stock ahead of a release.

Can't find what you're after? Email us directly at maison@chronique.au and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Privacy policy

Who we are. Chronique is operated by Maison Chronique Pty Ltd. Protecting your personal information matters to us, and this policy explains what we collect, why, and how it's used.

Information we collect. When you place an order or contact us, we collect things like your name, shipping and billing address, email address, phone number, and order history. Payment details are entered directly into Stripe, our payment processor, at checkout — we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it. We use this information to process and deliver your order, send confirmation and shipping emails, respond to enquiries and returns, and send you a "back in stock" alert if you've asked for one. If you've subscribed to our mailing list, we'll also send you updates on new arrivals and restocks — you can unsubscribe at any time.

Your shopping bag. The items in your bag are stored in your own browser (not on our servers) so they're there when you come back — this is cleared if you clear your browser data.

Sharing your information. We share personal information only where it's needed to run the business: with Stripe to process payments, with our email provider to send order and account emails, and with delivery carriers to get your order to you. We don't sell your personal information to anyone.

Where it's stored. We're based in Australia, but some of the services we rely on (like our payment and email providers) may process or store information on servers overseas, under their own privacy and security safeguards.

Data retention. We keep order records for as long as needed for tax, accounting, and other legal obligations.

Your rights. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and request that we correct or delete it, by emailing maison@chronique.au. We'll respond as quickly as we can.

Children's privacy. Chronique isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy from time to time as our business changes — the current version always applies. Last updated September 2026.
